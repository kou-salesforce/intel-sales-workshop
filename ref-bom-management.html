Module 2 Reference · Step-by-Step Training

Using the BOM Management Workbench

A hands-on guide for new users. Follow the numbered steps — each one shows exactly what to click and what you'll see.

Offline reference — no external links, safe behind the firewall
1

Open the workbench

Open your Opportunity, then click the Bill of Material tab, just above the record details. The BoM Builder loads: candidate components on the left, your Royal Silicon BoM on the right.

Tip: set “Selling as (Manufacturer)” at the top-right to your line — it decides which crosses count as “ours.”
2

Find candidate components

Pick any of the four ways to bring in parts:

  • Discover Components — auto-suggests parts for this design
  • Import BoM CSV — upload a Celus / KiCad export
  • Import BoM PDF — AI reads the document
  • Similar Opps / Z2Data tabs — pull from past deals or search the catalog
Parts appear in the left list. The same part is never added twice.
3

Read the candidate list

The pills at the top count your candidates three ways for the manufacturer you're selling as:

  • “N” — parts that are already ours
  • “N cross” — parts where we have a cross
  • “No N” — parts with no match yet

Each card shows the MPN, manufacturer, a match badge, and an Add to BoM button. Use the search box to filter by part, mfr or category.

4

See crosses, filter to “ours”

  • Click Show cross / alternative parts on any candidate.
  • Z2Data lists competitive equivalents, graded Drop-in A / B / C.
  • Click the persona pill to filter the list to just our equivalents — e.g. 24 of 50 crosses are Texas Instruments parts, each tagged “Ours.”
  • Add a cross straight to the BoM to swap in our part.
5

Open a part's detail

Click a part number — candidate, cross or BoM line — to open its detail. You get the full Z2Data profile: package, lifecycle, part score, years to EOL, RoHS / REACH / PFAS, country of origin, sellers, lead time, price — plus a cross-reference compatibility panel.

Everything you need to judge a part, without leaving the page.
6

Add to BoM & set UPA / SOM / Status

  • Click + Add to BoM on a candidate (or select several and add together).
  • It appears in the Bill of Materials on the right.
  • Set the numbers per line: UPA (units per assembly), SOM % (your share of that socket), Unit ASP (the price).
Forecast Revenue updates automatically from UPA × SOM × units.
7

Switch to Socket Mode for alternates

Use the BoM / Socket toggle at the top of the Bill of Materials. Device Mode shows one row per device — the simple view. Socket Mode groups several candidate devices under one socket; mark one as Primary, the rest are Alternatives you're still considering.

Why it matters: only the Primary carries a forecast line, so alternates never double-count. Promote an alternate and the forecast just re-routes. (Sample socket: one Primary — Bosch BMP390 — with two alternatives held under it.)
8

Read the component analysis

Scroll to the bottom of the workbench for the Bill of Materials Cascade. It tells you at a glance: silicon content per unit, total forecast revenue, and spend by category.

Use it to see which sockets carry the value and where to focus your design-in effort.
9

Generate a quote

  • Click Generate Quote in the Opportunity header.
  • The picker lists your quotable devices with role, status, forecast qty, UPA, SOM % and price.
  • Tick the devices to quote (or Select all). Won / Lost are hidden — toggle to include them.
  • Name the quote (optional) and click Generate Quote.
  • Salesforce builds the quote with a line per device and opens it — UPA and SOM carried across.
→ The native Quote opens — lines, totals and status ready.
Recap

The whole flow, in one place

  1. Open the Bill of Material tab and set your manufacturer persona
  2. Find candidates — discover, import CSV / PDF, or pull from past deals
  3. Read the candidate list and its Ours / cross / no-match pills
  4. Expand crosses and filter to our equivalents
  5. Open any part for full Z2Data detail
  1. Add to the BoM and set UPA, SOM % and status
  2. Use Socket Mode to hold alternates without double-counting
  3. Read the component analysis to see where the value is
  4. Generate a quote — without leaving the record
Everything writes back to standard Opportunity Products — so the native Salesforce forecast keeps working the whole time.
