The six steps

Build the forecast, click by click

1

Create the Opportunity

The Opportunity is the home for one deal — the forecast lives on it, so the demand signal enters exactly where you already work.

  • From the Opportunities tab, click New.
  • Fill Account, Name, Close Date, then add your Products — the chips you're selling become forecastable line items.
  • Set the Application field (e.g. “Consumer Webcam”) — this is the key that tells AI which demand model to use.
  • Enter Start date, Duration and total units, then Save.
You'll see a saved Opportunity with products and an Application — the foundation the AI forecast is built on.
2

Open the Workbench

The Workbench is the forecasting cockpit — it turns your opportunity into an editable, model-generated demand curve.

  • On the Opportunity record, click the Forecast Workbench tab.
  • Click Generate Forecast — AI draws a full demand curve across every month, no typing.
  • Read the curve up top and the month grid below it — the model's starting opinion, ready for your input.
You'll see a complete, model-generated demand curve you can upload to, reshape, or edit by hand.
3

Upload the customer sheet

Have the customer's own demand numbers in Excel? Don't retype them — upload the file and AI reads it into the Customer row.

  • Click Upload in the right-hand panel — a file picker opens (CSV, Excel, PDF or even a screenshot).
  • Choose the customer's file and confirm the month & quantity columns — AI parses even messy quarterly exports into a clean monthly series.
  • Review the preview, then click Apply — nothing commits until you approve it.
The customer's numbers land in the Customer row of the grid, ready to compare against the model.
4

Replicate with an AI prompt

No spreadsheet? Describe the demand in one plain-English sentence and AI builds the whole curve from scratch.

  • Click in the Ask AI to Reshape box.
  • Type the shape you want, e.g. “10,000,000 units over 3 years, gradual YoY growth, Black-Friday and early-summer peaks.”
  • Press Send and review the proposed curve — AI writes a structured plan; Apex computes every period exactly.
A full 36-month curve matching your description — generated in seconds, ready to fine-tune.
5

Nudge with follow-up prompts

The curve is a conversation — adjust it with more plain-English instructions and the math re-runs every time.

  • Type a refinement, e.g. “1st year 40%, distribute the rest over the remaining 2 years, same seasonal demand.”
  • Try shape nudges: “shift the peak to November” or “flatten by 20%” — every period recomputes; the total is preserved unless you change it.
  • Not what you meant? Click Undo — you can experiment freely, nothing is destructive.
A curve that matches your judgment, reached by talking to it — not by editing 36 cells by hand.
6

Commit each viewpoint

The grid keeps parallel rows — Customer, Sales, Sales Mgr, Predicted — so every opinion is preserved until actuals decide who was right.

  • Put the customer's number in the Customer row — their signal is captured, untouched by anyone else's edits.
  • Enter your own call in the Sales row — the number you're willing to stand behind.
  • Click Save to commit the Final row — your committed forecast rolls up to pipeline automatically.
Every viewpoint stored side-by-side — and later, actuals will show whose judgment was sharpest.
After the deal ships

Close the loop

The accuracy check

Click the Forecast Accuracy button on the Opportunity. A modal plots your guidance against actuals with accuracy, bias and correlation stats — then an Einstein analysis and event timeline tie the miss to real market events, not guesses.

Let AI recommend the correction

The modal hands you the exact Recommended prompt to correct the bias. Click Use this prompt — it drops into the Ask-AI-to-Reshape box — review and Send. A bias you'd have carried for quarters, corrected in two clicks.
