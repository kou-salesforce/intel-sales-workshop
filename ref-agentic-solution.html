The recurring gaps

Opportunity Structure

Sequential and high-maintenance — too much manual data upkeep.

Demand Forecast

A data schema, not a planning tool — lacks simulation and modeling; pre- vs. post-design-win blind spots.

BOM Management

Substitute parts, reference designs, and revenue tracking.

Quoting

Multidimensional & contracted pricing, pricing intelligence, bundles, multi-distributor / multi-location — and it takes too long to implement.

Partner Incentives

Onboarding, MDF, and the registration-to-debit process.

Design for Agentic AI

Three patterns, one spectrum

From the easiest adoption to the most open-ended — each pattern trades simplicity for reach.

Easiest adoption

Agent features in the app

Micro agents embedded directly in the user experience — the lowest-friction entry point.

Agentforce

Conversational & predictive

Hierarchical specialization and reusable components, driven from central Salesforce innovations.

Headless 360

Endless possibilities

Full data accessibility for agent processing — the most powerful, still dependent on the quality of the data.

Agentic coding is the game changer. It unlocks the user experience, changes the ROI for customization, makes ideas transferable, and scales — the leap from Salesforce Classic (2000) → Lightning (2015) → the Agentic UI (2024), now with an embedded micro agent.
